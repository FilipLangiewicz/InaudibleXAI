## First results (AST, ESC-50, 96 dB SPL, model 1)

Mean over 100 clips, with the 95 % bootstrap interval:

| Condition | Top-1 change | JS divergence |
|---|---|---|
| resynthesis | 0.00 | 0.000 |
| deletion | 0.33 [0.24, 0.42] | 0.139 [0.107, 0.174] |
| random_deletion | 0.92 [0.86, 0.97] | 0.635 [0.587, 0.681] |
| between_clips | 0.96 [0.92, 0.99] | 0.893 [0.863, 0.920] |

- The deletion removes 84 % of the bins and 16 % of the energy. The mean noise-to-mask ratio of the residual is -9.4 dB. The residual is above the threshold in 0.5 % of the bins.
- The deletion change is 16 % of the between-clip change. Thus this pair does not give the refuting result.
- Warning: in some clips, most of the deleted energy is below 100 Hz (rain: 73 %, sea waves: 64 %). The threshold in quiet is high there. Quiet clips (keyboard typing, peak -15 dBFS) lose much energy in the middle band. The listening test must check these clips first.
