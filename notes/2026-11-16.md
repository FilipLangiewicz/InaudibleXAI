## First check on a synthetic signal

- Signal: a 440 Hz tone with amplitude modulation and white noise (amplitude 0.02), 5 s, 96 dB.
- `mpeg1` deleted 79 % of the bins and 0.6 % of the energy. ViSQOL: 1.05.
- `lin_abdulla` deleted 44 % of the bins and 0.3 % of the energy. ViSQOL: 2.33.
- The low ViSQOL agrees with the band check of T1. The deletion of each bin of wide-band noise is audible in the sum over a band.
- The team must check this by ear (ABX test, issue #7).
- ViSQOL takes approximately 5.5 s for a clip of 5 s on one CPU core. Model 2 is approximately 9 times faster than model 1.

## Results of ast-esc50-v3abcd

AST on the 100 ESC-50 clips of T1. Mean over the clips, with the 95 % bootstrap interval.

| Variant | Masking model | Level | Bins deleted | Energy deleted | Deletion vs between (JS) | Verdict (JS) | ViSQOL of the deletion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v3a | mpeg1 | 96 dB | 84 % | 16 % | 15.5 % [11.9, 19.4] | outside | 3.26 [3.12, 3.40] |
| v3b | mpeg1 | 92 dB | 85 % | 17 % | 16.0 % [12.3, 19.9] | outside | 3.20 [3.06, 3.35] |
| v3c | lin_abdulla | 96 dB | 64 % | 6 % | 1.3 % [0.6, 2.2] | within | 3.69 [3.56, 3.81] |
| v3d | lin_abdulla | 92 dB | 66 % | 7 % | 1.6 % [0.9, 2.6] | within | 3.58 [3.44, 3.71] |

- v3a gives the same values as ast-esc50-v2. Thus the change of the code did not change model 1.
- The masking model changes the verdict for AST. Model 2 deletes less energy, and the JS change is within the between-clip control.
- With model 2, the top-1 measure does not agree with the JS measure. The top-1 class changes in 11 % [5, 17] of the clips. The ratio to the between-clip change is 12 % [5, 19], thus the verdict is "outside". The lower limit is near the margin of 5 %.
- The level of 92 dB changes the results only a little. The difference of 4 dB is small. Issue #12 must decide if this is a sufficient test of the level.
- ViSQOL of the deletion (3.2 to 3.7) is lower than ViSQOL of the random deletion (4.0 to 4.3). The random deletion changes the model output much more.
- A possible reason: the deletion removes the quiet background in complete bands, and ViSQOL compares log amplitudes in bands. The band check of T1 shows the same effect.
- Thus ViSQOL does not show that the deletion is inaudible. The ABX test (issue #7) must decide.
- The clips with the lowest ViSQOL under model 1: airplane (1.87), insects (1.90), door_wood_creaks (1.94).
- The job took 80 min on 4 CPU cores. The deletion and ViSQOL took 11 to 19 min. The AST inference took 58 min on one thread. Thus a GPU is better for the next job with a model.
