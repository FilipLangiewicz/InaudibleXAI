## Listening test (10 clips, ast-esc50-v2)

- Method: one team member played the original or the deleted clip. A second team member said which one it was.
- Result: 6 correct, 4 wrong. The one-sided binomial test against 50 % gives p = 0.38. The 95 % lower limit of the hit rate is 30 %.
- Thus 10 trials do not show an audible difference. They also do not show that the deletion is inaudible.
- The listener said that in the correct answers the deleted clip had less noise and less background. In some clips the listener heard no difference.
- This cue agrees with the band check: the deletion removes background noise in wide bands.
- Next test: more trials, an ABX design, and the same order of clips for each listener. With 20 trials, 15 correct answers show a difference (p < 0.05). With 40 trials, 26 correct answers are necessary.
